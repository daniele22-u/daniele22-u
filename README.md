Biomedical engineer working where **signals meet machine learning**: EEG, physiological time series, clinical data and medical images.
MSc in Biomedical Engineering (bioinformatics) at **Politecnico di Milano**, BSc at the University of Cagliari.

- 🧠 Writing my master's thesis on **deep learning for EEG decoding** (graph & hypergraph neural networks)
